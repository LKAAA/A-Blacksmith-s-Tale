Humans are of varying heights and builds, with diverse skin tones, hair, and eye colors. They are known for their adaptability and resilience, thriving in a wide range of environments. Despite having the shortest lifespans among the races, humans possess an unparalleled drive for innovation and progress. This relentless pursuit of growth often leads to conflicts with longer-living races, who may view human ambition as reckless or short-sighted. However, it is this very ambition that has propelled humanity to great heights, shaping the world in ways that no other race could imagine.

Humans are the most common race.
