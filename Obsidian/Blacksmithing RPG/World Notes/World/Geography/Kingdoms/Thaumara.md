##### Appendix

Thaumara is a magocracy. Lead by a council of the most powerful mages. Large magic academy. Area for Scholarly mages to be a part of. Large export is the recently created Magi-tech.

Capital: Nythalor
Demonym: Thaumaran
Languages: Common, Runic
Religions: Etherism (Followers of Ethria, known as the Goddess of the Ethereal. Shes the goddess of magic)
Type: Grand Magocracy
Allegiances: Friendly with Than, Neutral with The Central Kingdoms, Tense with Zentharis

The Ruling Party - The Council of Seven, comprised of seven of the most powerful mages of the current time. Decisions are made through debate and duels of intellect, not force or combat.
They reside in the center of Nythalor in a great tower.


Magi-tech has been recently discovered (within the past 5 years) and great academic excitement has been coursing through the kingdom. They hope to replace and enhance traditional industry with this new magic.
Alchemy and rune crafting is a highly  valued skillset, as well as adventuring mages beign requested all over the world recently ever since the dungeon bursts.
Numerous guilds of runic engravers and magic weilders dominate the economy

The capital has no dominant religion as alot of mages don't really believe in religion and tend to rely of wisdom and intellect

Nythalor - Capital City, home of the Council of Seven and the world's most prestigious magic academy.
Zarthuun - A port city in the north used for trade with The Central Kingdoms
Runebridge - Known for its colossal floating bridges powered by runic magic. 
Selvaris - A southern port city that commonly trades with Zentharis, Than, and Vashkara
