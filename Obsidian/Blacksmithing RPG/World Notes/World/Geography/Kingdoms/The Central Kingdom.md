The Central Kingdom
Alternate Names: 
The Holy Land
Size: ~120,000 million mi2
Population: ~24 million

The Kingdom is ruled by a Monarchy.
The governance of provinces is delegated to noble families.

The ranking of noble families is as so:
Monarch / Royal family 
Duke/Duchess - Rules a Duchy (large region)
Marquis/Marquess  - Rules a March (borderland region - responsible for defense)
Count/Countess - Rules a County (mid-sized region)
Baron/Baroness -  Rules a Barony (smaller land, often within a county)
Knights and Lords - Lesser nobles who serve under barons and counts.

Each of these nobles are vassals to their ruler and in return receive land and privileges.
Duke/Marquis/Count all swear fealty to the King.
Baron/Knights/Lords swear fealty to whomever they serve
If a noble fails their duty they can be striped of their land.

The Church also holds its own  land and can influence noble decisions
Some bishops and abbots hold noble titles and serve as vassals to the king.
