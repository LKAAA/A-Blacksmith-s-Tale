###### The Emergence Age
>[!caption|left]
>Main Article: [[The Emergence Age]]

<br>The First Age was a period spanning from 8000 [[Records of Time|BH]] to 4000 [[Records of Time|BH]]. It was the beginning of recorded time on [[Akora]], when it was just the [[Dwarves]] living deep in the continent and the [[Elves]] freely roaming in the forests of the surface. Much of the south of Akora was occupied by [[Elemental Dragons|Dragons]] that roamed the surface, though this was the time period before many Dragons became the [[Dracari]].<br>The Emergence age was a period from 4000 [[Records of Time|BH]] to around 2000 [[Records of Time|BH]]. It was a time where a great number of new races emerged on [[Akora]]. The [[Beast Races]] made their first emergence in this era, as well as many of the remaining [[Elemental Dragons|Dragons]] of the previous age becoming the [[Dracari]].
Deep in the south of Akora emerged the [[Monstrous Races]], including the [[Goblins]], [[Trolls]], and [[Orcs]]. While the Goblins preferred to stay in their deep labyrinths of caverns with the Trolls following suit, the [[Orcs]] preferred to spread out across the continent, making it as far as central Akora.

###### The Last Great Age
>[!caption|left]
>Main Article: [[The Last Great Age]]

<br>The Last Great Age, as it was known by the [[Elves]] begun at 2000 [[Records of Time|BH]] and ended at 1 [[Records of Time|BH]]. The end of this age is marked by the event known as the [[Advent of Humanity]], the first arrival of [[Humans]] on [[Akora]].
This age is marked as an era of peace. The great Elvish city of [[Sylhserin]] in the far north of Akora was at it's peak. The city of [[Yenthas]] was slowly growing during the latter half of this age.

###### The Present Age
>[!caption|left]
>Main Article: [[The Present Age]]

<br>The Present Age is sometimes known as the human age. It begun in the year 1 [[Records of Time|AH]], during what was known as the [[Advent of Humanity]] to the [[Elves]]. It was the arrival of the first [[Humans]] on the continent of [[Akora]]. It is considered one of the worst time periods in the history of Akora. 
It included many calamitous events such as the [[Destruction of Sylhserin]] in 294 [[Records of Time|AH]] and the [[Goblin Wars]] of 804 [[Records of Time|AH]]. The [[emergence of demons]] in 1094 [[Records of Time|AH]] was yet another calamity of this time period.
