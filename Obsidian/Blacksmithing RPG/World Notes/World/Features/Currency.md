The currency in this world is gold, silver, and bronze coins. 

**Exchange Rate:**
1 Gold coin per 10 Silver coins;
1 Silver coin per 10 Bronze coins;

**What is this money worth?**
Gold coins are valuable and used to buy higher-end equipment and magical items. 
A silver coin can get you a night at the cheapest inn, it is a laborer’s work for half a day, or a mile on a ship (roughly). 
A copper coin can purchase you a candle, a sack, or a whetstone as an example. A loaf of bread would be roughly 3 bronze coins. 
*based off of dnd*

*Other notes*

The [[The Empire|empire]] provides the money to the people.

You can earn money through labor, or commonly through seeking treasures in dungeons.

Money can secure you food, shelter, and comforts, or buy you passage to distant lands and exotic wares.
