The Cult of Dotthar originates from Westios, but has slowly spread into other regions of Akora. Dotthar is the [[The Zentharis Pantheon|Westios Pantheon's]] god of chaos. The Cult of Dotthar is separated into multiple sects that all follow his word independently and claim to have been spoken to by Dotthar themselves. 

##### Sects
- [[The Black Sun]]
- The Hands of Ruin
- The Unrisen Sun

##### Hierarchy
- Some call them the Hands of Dotthar, others the Harbingers of Chaos, others still Prophets. This is the highest rank that claims to speak directly to Dotthar. 
- The whispered - below the prophet in command, but also claims to have heard Dotthar’s voice. 
- Ashwalkers - the people who do the dirty work
- Followers - everyone else

##### Beliefs
- One day Dotthar will come down to Saelis and unshackle the followers from the bounds of this world. He will provide strength to all who give themselves to him. 
- To serve is to be reborn and being reborn grants power. 

##### Symbols
- Broken chains
- A black sun 

##### Rituals/Practices
- Sacrifices 
- A type of magic to inflict hallucinations on the mind
- Hallucinogens


### Location of Sects
[[The Black Sun]] is currently located in Vashkara.
