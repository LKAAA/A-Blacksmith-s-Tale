The Black Sun is a sect of the [[Cult of Dotthar]] located and working in [[Vashkara]]. Their goal is to take control of the [[Grand Duke Silian Vashteria]] thus gaining control of Vashkara as a whole. They plan on using this control to manipulate the Grand Duke into attack [[Westios]] with the end goal of defeating Westios's empress, the god [[Ivphyn]], counterpart of [[Dotthar]], goddess of order.

The leader of the Black Sun, the Hand of Dotthar as he calls himself, is the main advisor of [[Grand Duke Silian Vashteria]].
