Adventurers are members of the [[Adventurer's Guild]]. Anyone can become an adventurer after reaching the age of 15. Adventurers take and complete [[Quests|quests]] that have been assigned to them from the guild. They are the main force that prevent dungeon monsters from overflowing onto the surface. 

Adventurers are ranked on a scale of E to S, based on factors such as level, mana capacity, and strength. The weakest rank adventurer is E, then it is D, C, B, A, and finally the strongest rank S. S rank adventurers are very rare with only a few being active in the world at any time.
