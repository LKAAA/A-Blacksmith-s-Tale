Flowing mana is the physical form of [[Mana|mana]], a force that fills every object at a molecular level. Little is known about how flowing mana comes to be but springs of it have been found in numerous different places, from hidden mana oasis in forests, to the deepest depths of [[Dungeons|dungeons]]. 
Flowing mana can be generated by high level [[Mage|mages]], but it is extraordinarily difficult to do as it places great strain on the innate connect between person and mana. Overproduction of this flowing mana can be fatal as it consumes a tremendous amount of mana, and as such it is more common to see it sought out and imported from [[Flowing Mana Springs|flowing mana springs]]. 
Flowing mana can be used and harnessed in two main ways. The first is usage in infusing objects with mana. This usage is most common in [[Rune Magic]], as it is the easiest way for [[Runesmiths]] with no mana capabilities to infuse their creations with mana to activate the runes. 
The second usage for flowing mana is mana recovery. When a mage uses magic it uses some of the mana in their body. This mana regenerates automatically over time but can be sped up by bathing in flowing mana, although bathing for too long can cause your body to obtain too much mana causing damage to your [[Mana Core|mana core]].
