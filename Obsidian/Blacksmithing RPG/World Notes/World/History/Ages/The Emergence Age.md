The Emergence Age was a period in [[Akora]]'s history from 4000 [[Records of Time|BH]] to around 2000 [[Records of Time|BH]]. It was a time where a great number of new races emerged on [[Akora]]. The [[Beast Races]] made their first emergence in this era, as well as many of the remaining [[Elemental Dragons|Dragons]] of the [[The First Age|previous age]] becoming the [[Dracari]].
Deep in the south of Akora emerged the [[Monstrous Races]], including the [[Goblins]], [[Trolls]], and [[Orcs]]. While the Goblins preferred to stay in their deep labyrinths of caverns with the Trolls following suit, the [[Orcs]] preferred to spread out across the continent, making it as far as central Akora.
