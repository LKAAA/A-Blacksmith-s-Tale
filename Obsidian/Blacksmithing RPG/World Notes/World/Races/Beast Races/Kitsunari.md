Kitsunari are cunning and clever beings, bearing the likeness of humanoids, with fox-like features They possess slender, agile humanoid bodies, with pointed ears, bushy tails, and eyes that sparkle with intelligence and curiosity. Kitsunari are intelligent creatures that are naturally able to perform great magic. Able to shift between their human-like form and fox form, Kitsunari are creatures of adaptability and versatility, able to thrive in both urban environments and the depths of the wilderness.
