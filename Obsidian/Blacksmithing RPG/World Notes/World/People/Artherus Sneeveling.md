A [[Humans]] [[Academic Mages|Academic mage]]. He was the first to discover [[Rune Magic|runes]], and begin translating the ancient language that it comes from.

He died before finishing his work, but not before revealing some of what he had translated. He revealed multiple runes as how to use them.

Before he died it was rumored that he talked of a great discovery that would revolutionize magic itself, but this is little more than a rumor. No notes have been found on this would be discovery and any discovery that he made seems to have died with him.  Rumors of his long lost journal frequently circulate through the [[Runesmiths|runesmith]] community, but this journal has never been confirmed to exist.
