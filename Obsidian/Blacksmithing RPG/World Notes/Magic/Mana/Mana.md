Mana flows through every living thing, but only some can tap into this force and use it directly. Along with it being inside of every living being at a molecular level, it also overflows into the world in a form coined [[Flowing Mana]]. It is unknown why this happens but it can be harnessed to great effects. 
Mana is used to [[Casting Magic|cast magic]] and refers to the form inside of a being. A mana user or [[Mage|mage]] as they are frequently called can directly tap into this mana that flows through them and give it form. The way they give it form can differ greatly and depends on the user's skill level, knowledge, and innate mana capabilities. 
Each person has a certain amount of mana capability, or to put it another way, a certain amount of mana stored in their body at anytime. This can be increased through practice and casting magic. Casting magic will temporarily use the mana in your body, but your body can automatically restore this mana over time, typically while resting. 
There are adverse effects to using too much mana at once. Depleting all of the mana stored in your body can cause irreparable damages to what is known as your [[Mana Core|mana core]].
Being able to use mana directly is not a learnable skill, but it is a trainable skill. You can improve at being able to cast magic and use mana, but you cannot learn how to use mana if you were not born with it.
