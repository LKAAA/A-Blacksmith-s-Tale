“I am the setting sun! I am the final horizon! I am the fate of all things! I. Am. The. End.”
“All things fade into twilight, all paths lead to dusk, but my light is eternal.”

The empress' full title is "The God Empress of the Setting Sun". 
She proclaims herself as [[Ivphyn]], the goddess of law and order, the queen of the heavens descended down to [[Akora]] to lead her people against the god chaos and demons, [[Dotthar]]. Unknown to the people of [[Overview of Westios|Westios]] she is a false god who is simply a very powerful [[S-Rank]] [[Mage]] gifted in light magic. 

No living being is allowed to see her face (in public),  and all nobles must wear blindfolds in his presence, lest they burn to death from her power. 

The people of Westios believe her to be immortal, after all she has been ruling for hundreds of years, but the truth is that she is being replaced before her death by her children, which no one knows about. These children are kept secret and put through grueling tests and brain washings to turn them into the perfect Ivphyn. No one knows about this because they are not allowed to see her face. 

TLDR: She is a powerful light mage impersonating the Queen of the Heavens, and raising children through brainwashing to take her place.
