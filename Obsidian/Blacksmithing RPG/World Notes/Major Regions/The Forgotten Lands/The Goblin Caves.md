Massive underground catacomb like caves. Like a labyrinth. 
The goblins have different main caves each ruled by a hobgoblin, like a clan or tribe type thing.
They don't like humans and frequently emerge from their caves in human raiding parties. 
They have made some underground caverns that go further out from the Forgotten lands, into some kingdoms neighboring kingdoms.
50 years ago the goblin clans were unified by the [[Dakad the Goblin Herald]]. He formed the goblins into [[The Great Goblin Army]]. They waged war against the human kingdom but were ultimately pushed back by the the [[S-Rank]] hero [[Thorin Ragnorok]], a Dwarven [[Fighter]].
