The Tale of Nard the Mapmaker

Nard owned a little shop that sold maps in the human kingdom. For 30 years he attempted to map out the wild forests to the east, but each one always ended in gibberish. The pathways overlapped, seemingly loop into themselves, but never crossing, nor leading anywhere. One day, when he was nearing the end of his time, he set out on one last journey. After yet another week of pathways to nowhere a lone elf emerged in front of him. 
"For the last 30 years I have seen you come here with your maps. Why do you still come back after finding nothing?"
Nard thought for a moment, then laughed. "I knew the only thing that can't be mapped is someone who is really good at hiding stuff. I just wanted to be the first to find it."
The elf stared at him, before motioning for Nard to follow him. Nard was shown the hidden city of the elves, and finally achieved his goal. He stayed there until the end of his days, before finally dying in his newly found elven bed.
