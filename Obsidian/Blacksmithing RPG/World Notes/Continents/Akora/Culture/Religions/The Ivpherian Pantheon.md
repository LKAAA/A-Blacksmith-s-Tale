### **The Primordial God**

Ianis (Ee-on-is) - The void. (He is the void. He isn’t the god of the void, or hold dominion over it, he simply is the void)
### **Major Gods**

[[Ivphyn]] (Eye-phin) - Goddess of law, order, sky, light. Queen of the Heavens.

Ydis (ye-dis) God of peace, art, music, theater, poetry, writing.

[[Dotthar]] (Do-th-are) - God of chaos and demons. King of the Underworld.

Nehelia (Ne-heel-ee-a) - Goddess of magic, inevitability, necessity.

Aelia (A-leah) - Goddess of love, beauty, marriage, heirs, home, domesticity, childbirth.

Faeros (Fae-ros) - God of promiscuity, parties, festivals, madness, drunkenness, ecstasy, desire, pleasure.

Belis (Bel-ees) Goddess of war, courage, battle strategy, warfare.

Fretum (Fray-tum) - God of the sea, rivers, floods, droughts.

Cohas (Co-hos) - God of fire, blacksmiths, forge, metalworking, crafts.

Itia (E-tia) - Goddess of the earth and nature, spring, summer, vegetation.

Actus (Act-oos) - God of grain, agriculture, harvest, growth, nourishment, plague.

Azanh (A-zan) God of wealth, money, luck, and greed.

Lunis (Loo-nis) - Goddess of hunt, wilderness, animals, moon.

Puella (Pay-Luh) - Goddess of chastity, maidens, young girls, sun.

Pelus (Pe-lus) - God of reason, wisdom, intelligence, prophecy.

### **Creation of the Gods**

Ianis created Fretum and Itia first thus creating the earth with its seas and land. Then he created the twins Aelia and Belis, bringing forth love and war into the world. He then created Ivphyn and Ydis as well as the heavens, bringing law and order, the sky, and peace into existence. After came Dotthar and Necesse, the balance to Ivphyn and Ydis, and the underworld.

Fretum and Itia, the earth and the seas, married and gave birth to Actus and Cohas, agriculture and fire.

Ivphyn and Ydis married and gave birth to Lunis, Puella, and Pelus. Lunis brought animals and the moon, Puella the sun, and Pelus reason, wisdom, and prophecy. 

Finally Dotthar and Necesse married and gave birth to Faeros and Azanh, bringing ecstasy, pleasure, parties, wealth, and greed into existence.

###  Creation of the World

##### Year 0 
Nothingness and the void

##### Year 1 
Ianis, the void, gives shape to his creations, Fretum and Itia, thus creating Saelis, the earth, and the seas, filling the void with something. Then he created the twins Aelia and Belis, bringing forth love and war into the world. Ianis then created Ivphyn and Ydis, bringing law and order, the sky, and peace into existence, and finally he made Dotthar and Necesse, the balance to Ivphyn and Ydis.

##### Years 2-10 
Fretum and Itia, the earth and the seas, married and gave birth to Actus and Cohas, agriculture and fire.

Ivphyn and Ydis married and gave birth to Lunis, Puella, and Pelus. Lunis brought animals and the moon, Puella the sun, and Pelus reason, wisdom, and prophecy. 

Finally Dotthar and Necesse married and gave birth to Faeros and Azanh, bringing ecstasy, pleasure, parties, wealth, and greed into existence. 

##### Years 10 - 500
For the next 500 years the gods continued to live in relative peace, with only a few squabbles here and there, until one day, Cohas decided that he no longer wished to work the forges alone. He attempted to create a helper for himself, one he called the Dwarf.  And thus ended the first great age of Saelis, and begun the second great age of Saelis. 

Cohas, enamored with the shape of his creation decided that just having a shell to do whatever he wanted was not very interesting, so he first approached Pelus, son of Ivphyn. “Lo’ Pelus,” he said. “Grant my creation your gift of intelligence so that they might create unique things like myself.”

Pelus obliged. 

Cohas then visited the twins, intending to speak with Aelia alone, but that was never easy, love lurks around war after all. 

“Lo’ Aelia, grant my creation the gift of love, marriage, and childbirth, so that they may go on to become a great number of intelligent beings.

“I would love to,” said Aelia, “What great fun this all sounds, but what is love without having to fight for it! Oh Belis you must come too and grant Cohas’ lovely creation your gift!”

With both Pelus and Aelia’s gift acquired (and now Belis too), Cohas believed his little Dwarf creation has almost everything they needed. All that was left was a home and a little gift from himself. He granted them fire, a gift he knew would do great things.  

Finally to grant them a home he decided on a place near to his own. 

“Lo mother,” said Cohas, approaching Itia Goddess of the earth. “Allow me to place my creation deep in the earth, so that they may have a place to stay while creating everyday.”

Itia obliged, and with that a new race was placed beneath the continent of [[Akora]] a race that was named the [[Dwarves|Dwarf]].

Actus, hearing of his brother's success, was jealous. He too wished to create something to impress. But he had not the skills to forge out of stone, instead just the talent grow the unknown. He created his own being, one he called the [[Elves|Elf]]. His perfect creation, the counterpart of the Dwarf. While the Dwarf was short, the Elf was tall, where the Dwarf was stout, the Elf was delicate, and where the Dwarf was bulky, the Elf was skinny. 

Like his brother he visited each of the gods, to gift his new creation with intelligence and thought, and then onwards to the gift of love and life. But Actus was not one to lose to his brother, and while Cohas gave his creation fire and invention, Actus wanted his gift to them to be better. 

“Necesse oh Necesse, he called, please grant my little Elves just a touch of your magic!”

Rising from the ground she laughed, “Little Actus calling me for a favor? A gift like that will require quite the pact.”

“Anything, anything,” Actus said, content on giving all to make his brother fall. 

“Anything you say? You seem a little fey, but I’ll grant your request so long as you grant mine in turn. Go to Saelis, your mother's body, there is just one little thing you have to get for me. To the east of the continent Akora, stands a great tree, simply take this blade and stab it in deep. The sap will flow, simply grab a cup for me, and my gift will appear before thee.”

And Actus agreed, before thinking twice, he grabbed the knife, all ready to slice. To the tree he went and once there he did all that was asked. With a great plunge he stabbed into the tree, and at once he heard a great scream. Before him appeared his mother in pain, with glowing white blood streaming from her chest. 

“My son, how could you? Why would you do this?”

From within the tree flowed the same glowing blood, her life force linked to the land she created. From inside his head he heard a little laugh. 

“Now place your little Elf, in the gift from me.”

A trick he realized, so blinded with greed, he’d betrayed his mother, all for wanting to beat his brother.

“I was tricked mother!” Actus said, taking a step forward to reach his mother.

“This pain is great, tell me what blade you have used for this.”

“I do not know, Necesse gave it to me-“

His mother’s eyes widened with great fear, something he had yet to see before. 

“Why have you done this my son, you know Necesse wants my blood for her magic.”

“I just wanted a bit of her magic for my new creation. Mother look, it’s greater than my brother’s own innovation.”

“All this for a petty score, for your greed and your pride you have betrayed me.”

“No mother, it was an accident, I didn’t mean for this to happen, just to give my creation a little magic.”

Swaying on her feet, Itia looked pale, the blood flowing from her had yet to quail. 

“I must go rest for your blade has cursed me.” And with a twirl she disappeared, white blood still flowing deep from within the tree. 

And this is the tale of how flowing mana came to be, and along with it, the Elves who were born intertwined with it. 

For the next 1000 years the Elves and the Dwarves lived separately on Akora.

##### Year 1502

In the year 1502 the gods decided to create something new. Tired of only watching the Dwarves and the Elves who lived secluded from one another they created new beings. Among these new beings were the Kitsunari, the Nekojin, the Taurians, the Dracari (in their elemental form), the Goblins, the Orcs, and finally most intriguing of all, the humans. The gods placed them an Akora and set them free, gifting each of them the same as Cohas had given the Dwarves. 

##### Year 1535

In peace they all lived, after all they had nothing to fight over, but Dotthar quickly grew tired of this. Watching them all live in peace in harmony it reminded him all too much of that moron Ydis. I know he thought to himself, I should cause a little chaos. 

He gathered his children and a few others as well, and together convinced them how much fun it would be to give each little creation a few more gifts. 

Not wanting to see their creations just destroy themselves Ivphyn granted each creation the gift of law and order, and Ydis granted them peace, and the arts, and Pelus finally granted them wisdom, when before they had only intelligence. 

And this is the story of how of how chaos, greed, ecstasy, desire, but also law, order, peace,  and wisdom was granted to the creations on Akora, thus beginning the third great age of Saelis.
