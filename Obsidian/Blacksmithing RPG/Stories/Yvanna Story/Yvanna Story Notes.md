Option 1

He looked her up and down, seemingly measuring her. 

“Hmph.”

Hmph? What does hmph mean? Yvanna’s frantic thoughts were interrupted by the Duke. 

“You just recently turned sixteen, correct?” 

“Yes… sir,” replied Yvanna, unsure how to respond exactly.

“Very well then, I believe it is finally time for you to study in the ways of nobility.”

Yvanna’s mouth dropped. She quickly closed it, unable to believe what her ears had just heard. Have I gone crazy and started hearing things? She thought to herself.

“Wh-What do you mean… sir?” questioned Yvanna still not trusting her own hearing. 

“You will learn the proper ways of a noblewoman. Etiquette, history, dancing. Then when the time is right I will fully instate you as one of my daughters.” 

Yep, Yvanna was sure of it now. She had gone crazy. She pinched herself trying to get out of the obvious dream she was in. This did nothing but make her arm sting a little. If this isn’t a dream surely it's a joke then right? She thought to herself. Yvanna shuffled her feet trying to find the right words to say. 

“Um, why… do you want me to become a noble?”

“Well you are my daughter. It only makes sense for you to study to become a noble.”  
“But my mother is of low birth. I thought that meant that I was the same as a commoner.”

“My blood in you is enough to make you far greater than a commoner.”

Yvanna didn’t know that’s how it worked with nobility, she had only ever assumed that she had worked as a maid because of her common blood. 

“Since you are to be instated as one of my daughters,” the Duke continued. “You will of course be required to marry. You may not know this but that is one of the main jobs of a young noblewoman. You will bring great honor to this house.”

Yvanna’s head was starting to hurt. First she was told that she had to become a noblewoman now she's finding out she has to marry as well? Marriage had never even crossed her mind before. A few nagging questions still lurked in the back of her mind. Why only now is this happening? Why when nobles study for their entire lives, is she only starting now? Why had he finally chosen her?

Option 2

  

“You will marry Count Galean Vane of Frostguard.”

What? What does he mean I’m going to marry a count? A maid marrying a count is unheard of. Why would he need me to marry a count when he has two pure blood daughters to do it? 

“You will learn proper etiquette and the ways of a noble woman,” he continued. 

Yvanna’s head was beginning to hurt. This was all so confusing. Her father needed her to marry the count so she could give him information? Why now all of the sudden? Was this really something she could do better than her sisters? They had been studying to be noble ladies for their entire lives after all, while she had only been working as a maid. Why had he chosen her?

“Um, Duke- er- father. I-If I may, wouldn’t one of my sisters be a better fit to marry a count?”

His sharp gaze pierced through Yvanna, but she was unable to glean anything from his expression. 

“Perhaps. But I think you can become the perfect fit for this role.” 

“Thank you father. I will do my best.”

Yvanna struggled to hide the smile that fought to emerge. Her father’s words had thrilled her. He thought that she could become a noble woman?

“When will the marriage ceremony take place father?” Yvanna asked.

“It will take place one year from now in early summer. It’s one of the few times Frostguard is accessible,” said father. He began to shuffle with his papers on desk again signaling that the conversation was coming to a close. He looked back up at Yvanna. “Until then you will stay in the left wing next to your sisters. Claude will guide you to your new room.”

Yvanna dipped into a curtsy in front of her father.  
“Yes father, thank you father.”

She turned to leave but as she reached the door she stopped, her hand still on the handle. Turning, she said:

“Um, father. What of my mother? Where will she stay?” 

He glanced up at her. His expression changed for a moment before quickly returning to the serious, almost glare that had graced his face for the entire meeting up until that point. 

“She will continue working in the kitchens.” He once again turned to his desk picking up a pen to jot something down. 

Yvanna stood at the door for a moment. She really only wanted to know where her mother would go when she left for Frostguard. She didn’t really understand how these noble marriages worked, how often she got to come back home, or if her mother got to visit her. Yvanna supposed that she would learn that over the next year. A little nagging thought pulled at her brain. I still don’t understand why I am suddenly being chosen to do this job. But now wasn’t the time for these thoughts. She had finally talked to her father, and he needed her! 

“Thank you father.” Once again Yvanna lightly curtsied as her mother had taught her before turning swiftly and leaving through the door, shutting it behind her. Claude stood beside the door as she walked out. He quickly turned and started off down the hallway without so much as a glance to her. They arrived at a large door after a few minutes of walking. Claude opened it and beckoned her inside. The room was far bigger than any room she had ever stayed in. Two large windows adorned the back wall and in-between them stood a large bed. To the right when entering the room was a large dresser and a full length mirror, while on the left wall was a small couch and a desk with a matching chair. 

“You will be informed of where to go for your studies tomorrow. Until then you have been instructed to get comfortable with your room.” 

“O-ok,” stuttered Yvanna. Without so much as another glance at her, he abruptly stepped out of the room and shut the door. She heard his footsteps fading from outside as she looked around.

# This is more of a story synopsis than anything

  

Duke Teiros Mercili is married to Countess Eliri of the Caervan family. Together they have 3 children, one son and two daughters. Duke Teiros, ever the fool for beautiful women, fell for a young maid that worked in his estate named Yanni. She soon gave birth to a child which she called Yvanna. Countess Elira soon found out about this bastard child and became furious. Referring to them as a stain on the perfect Mercili bloodline she demanded they be removed.

  

Not wanting word of his infidelity to come out to the public but still unwilling to kill a direct descendant of the Mercili family (tainted blood of a commoner though she had), Duke Teiros sent Yanni and Yvanna to work in the the depths of the kitchen where they would not be seen by others. 

  

Yvanna worked hard and strove for love and acknowledgment from her family that only treated her as a tool and a mistake. Her mother was the only one that showed her actual love through her early years. 

  

Duke Teiros the ever ambitious man set his eyes upon Frostguard - The key to the north. Whoever controlled Frostguard controlled the north. The Count of Frostguard had no wife and Duke Teiros saw this as a perfect opportunity. If he married one of his daughters he could use them to claim Frostguard from the inside. 

  

But Duchess Elira was against this plan. She did not wish for her children to travel that far into the cold and dreary north, nor to marry a simple count with many unsavory rumors. “I know, what if we send Yvanna. She would be perfect for this role with a little training.”

  

Duke Teiros summoned Yvanna to him. “You will marry Count Frostguard,” he told her coldly. “and will send reports on everything that happens in Frostguard. If you do this I may even officially recognize you as my daughter.”

  

Ecstatic at the prospect of finally being accepted into the family Yvanna naively accepted this proposal with little complaint. She was trained to act the part of a noblewoman—but the “training” was harsh and abusive. She was beaten for minor errors, starved for disobedience, and humiliated daily by the Duchess and her daughters.

  

Finally Yvanna was sent away to the north to marry the count. 

But Count Galean Frostguard was not the monster she had been warned about. He was cold and awkward, yes—but it was the cold of a man who had long been alone, not the cold of the rumored cruel man. Beneath his stony silence was a man of discipline, principle, and unexpected gentleness. Over time Galean opened up to her—and to her surprise, she began to care for him and eventually fell in love.

But she had already begun sending letters back to her father, reporting on the Count’s strengths, weaknesses, and defenses. Desperate to be accepted into the family she had always sought acknowledgment from she had thought she was being loyal to the thing that would bring her happiness and fulfillment.

While Yvanna was away, Yanni - her mother - suddenly fell ill. She passed away shortly after. The news shattered Yvanna. But the worst news came when returning home for her mother’s funeral, where she overheard the first daughter of Duchess Elira mention her mother’s plan to rid the family of the maid that seduced her husband. Yvanna finally realized that she would never fit in this family. She realized that she had been used from the beginning, that the Duke never saw her as a daughter, only a pawn. 

When Duke Teiros demanded more detailed reports. He threatened to expose her early letters if she disobeyed. Yvanna decided to disobey anyway. She stopped writing the letters. She chose Galean over her family that never loved her.

In revenge, Duke Teiros sent a sealed letter directly to the Count, exposing Yvanna’s early betrayal.

But Galean knew of this already. After all there was only one reason a Duke would marry his daughter (bastard or not) to a mere Count in the North. At first on guard against her he slipped her false information to feed her father. Slowly over time they fell for each other. 

When the Duke’s plans to seize Frostgate accelerated, Yvanna, together with Galean, hatched a plan to catch the Duke red handed in his treasonous bid for power. (Somehow get this evidence idk yet) They smuggled the evidence to the King of Than. The King, eager to weaken the Mercili Dukedom and finally having the evidence to do so, acted swiftly.

Duke Teiros was denounced. He lost vast holdings in the south, his political allies fled, and the title of Duke was stripped. House Mercili fell into disgrace and disrepair, and Duchess Elira, removed of status and influence, vanished from court life.
