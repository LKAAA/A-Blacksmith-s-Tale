Since the destruction of [[Cerfan|Northern and Southern Cerfan]], the longest remaining records of time are stored by both the [[Elves]] and [[Dwarves]] located on the continent of [[Akora]]. As such these records are centered on the major event of humans arriving on the continent of Akora. This is known as BH (Before Humans) and AH (After Humans). BH counts down to zero while AH counts up from zero.
