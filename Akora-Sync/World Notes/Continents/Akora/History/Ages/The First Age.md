The First Age spanned from 12000 [[Records of Time|BH]] to 8000 [[Records of Time|BH]]. This age was dominated by the ancient civilization called [[Relva’kan]]. Although not much is still known about them in the current day, stories have been told of their cities reaching all of the way into the stars and all the way through the earth to the other side. It was a strong civilization until it befell what was called [[The Destruction of Relva’kan]]
In the Labyrinth like cities under the ground, monsters appeared. They emerged from every hole that existed and yet more that were created. They poured out of the walls wreaking havoc throughout the cities. Not knowing what to do the Elders of Relva’kan decided to seal these the entrances of these cities, trapping the monsters but also the people of Relva’kan’s underground cities. Many of the sealed people were slaughtered, but some, after being trapped for centuries on end, went mad, twisting into horrid creatures called [[Demons]]. These underground cities would eventually be refereed to as [[Dungeons]], and they still remain scattered throughout Akora. This event was called the first great calamity.

The second Great Calamity occurred soon after, when the earth quaked and shook with power stronger than had ever been seen before. The towering cities that seemingly touched the stars crumbled and fell.
