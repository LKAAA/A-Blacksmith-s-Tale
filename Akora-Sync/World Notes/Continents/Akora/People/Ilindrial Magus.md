An ancient [[S-Rank]] [[Mage]]. He is most known for his sacrifice to save the town [[Aldorin]] from 3 [[Elemental Dragons]]. By overloading his mana core he caused a great explosion that annihilated both the dragons and his own core causing his death. 

Excerpt from *[[Tales of the Ancient Heroes]]*, by [[Pagus Thinsly]]: "O' hero of old Ilindrial Magus, renowned for his great deed of sacrifice, a hero to all. Slayer of the Elemental Drakes, savior of Aldorin. Through his core begot peace, may his name be forever praised."
