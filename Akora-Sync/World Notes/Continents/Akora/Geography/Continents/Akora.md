Akora (pronounced: Ah-core-ah) is a major continent on the planet [[Saelis]]. The current year of Akora is 401 [[Records of Time|AH]].

#### Geography

###### Northwest
Once a region of lush forests, it now lies in an eternal winter. This region is generally called "The Northern Reaches", and merges with the region to the east of it. It is currently mostly uninhabited besides a few small tribes. 
Located in the center of this north-western region is the ruins of the [[Elves|elven]] city [[Sylhserin]], which was destroyed by a [[Nothern Reaches Dungeon Unsealing|dungeon being accidentally unsealed]] in 325 [[Records of Time|AH]].
Contains: 
- The [[Pale Tundra]]
- Ruins of [[Sylhserin]]
- The [[Unsealed Dungeon of the North]]

###### North
A continuation of [[The Northern Reaches]], the north contains a sub region of it called the [[Pale Tundra]]. The Pale tundra is an expansive plain of snow surrounded by the [[Wintermaw Peaks]], a massive chain of mountains that act as a sort of wall between the eternal winter of the Northern Reaches and the rest of Akora. 
Contains:
- The [[Pale Tundra]]
- The [[Wintermaw Peaks]]

###### Northeast
The north-east does not lay in eternal snow, but instead sits on the other side of the [[Wintermaw Peaks]]. The entire north-east is covered in a massive forest called the [[The Elderwoods|Elderwoods]]. This ancient forest hides the elven city called [[Yenthas]]. An ancient magic surrounds this region hiding Yenthas, and causing the region to be mostly unexplored by those who do not know of the city. 
Contains:
- The [[The Elderwoods|Elderwoods]]
- [[Yenthas]]
- [[Sealed Dungeon of the Elderwoods]]

###### West
This region includes the empire of [[Ivpheria]], located from the western coast of the [[Ocean of Glass]], across the [[Golden Steppe]], all the way to the [[Duskridge Mountains]]. Further south along the western coast lies the [[Shattered Isles]], a once bustling island that was shattered into isles when a dungeon was unsealed.
Contains:
- The [[Ocean of Glass]]
- The [[Golden Steppe]]
- The [[Duskridge Mountains]]
- The [[Shattered Isles]]
- The Empire of [[Ivpheria]]

#### History

The history of Akora spans for several thousand years. 

###### The First Age
>[!caption|left]
>Main Article: [[The First Age]]

<br>The First Age spanned from 12000 [[Records of Time|BH]] to 8000 [[Records of Time|BH]]. This age was dominated by the ancient civilization called [[Relva’kan]]. Although not much is still known about them in the current day, stories have been told of their cities reaching all of the way into the stars and all the way through the earth to the other side. It was a strong civilization until it befell what was called [[The Destruction of Relva’kan]]
In the Labyrinth like cities under the ground,  monsters appeared. They emerged from every hole that existed and yet more that were created. They poured out of the walls wreaking havoc throughout the cities. Not knowing what to do the Elders of Relva’kan decided to seal these the entrances of these cities, trapping the monsters but also the people of Relva’kan’s underground cities. These underground cities would eventually be refereed to as [[Dungeons]], and they still remain scattered throughout Akora. This event was called the first great calamity.

The second Great Calamity occurred soon after, when the earth quaked and shook with power stronger than had ever been seen before. The towering cities that seemingly touched the stars crumbled and fell. 

###### The Fallen Age
>[!caption|left]
>Main Article: [[The Fallen Age]]

<br>The Fallen Age is a period that spanned from 8000 [[Records of Time|BH]] to 5000 [[Records of Time|BH]]. The Fallen Age was a time of darkness, with little to no records of history. Following the [[The Destruction of Relva’kan|Destruction of Relva’kan]] there was no dominant civilization that ruled the land. Instead [[Elemental Dragons]] ruled the land. The strongest of these were Der’sal, the great black dragon, Sun’lin the golden dragon, Bel’yin the rock drake, and Sel’Ven the serpent drake. Each of these dragons controlled the north, east, south, and west respectively. Many other dragons roamed the lands but each followed one of the four great dragons of the time. 

###### The Emergence Age
>[!caption|left]
>Main Article: [[The Emergence Age]]

<br>The Emergence Age spanned from 8000 [[Records of Time|BH]] to 4000 [[Records of Time|BH]]. It was the beginning of the current period of recorded time on [[Akora]]. Though the [[Elemental Dragons]] still ruled Akora other civilizations began to emerge. The first of these were the dwarves and the elves. Though they had been around since nearly the time of Relva’kan they had not been a civilization of note until this time. The Dwarves began to build great underground cities, while the Elves began to create intricate cities in the forests of Akora. They Dragons, interested in the small people creating things on Akora, encouraged them and Sun’lin the golden dragon even become a great ally of the elves. 
The second civilizations to emerge occured in the south when a weakening seal on on of the Dungeons of Relva’kan burst, releasing the trapped beings inside. These were the goblins, trolls, and orcs. The orcs created cities and towns on the surface first in the south, before beginning to expand to the north. The trolls and goblins stayed in the south, hiding under the vast mountain ranges, in tunnel systems that they created. 
Finally near the end of this age the beast-tribes of [[Nislia]] made their way onto the main continent of Akora and began roaming the lands. 

###### The Last Great Age
>[!caption|left]
>Main Article: [[The Last Great Age]]

<br>The Last Great Age, as it was known by the [[Elves]] began at 4000 [[Records of Time|BH]] and ended at 1 [[Records of Time|BH]]. The end of this age is marked by the event known as the [[Advent of Humanity]], the first arrival of [[Humans]] on [[Akora]].
This age is marked as an era of peace. The great Elvish city of [[Sylhserin]] in the far north of Akora was at it's peak. The city of [[Yenthas]] was slowly growing during the latter half of this age.

###### The Present Age
>[!caption|left]
>Main Article: [[The Present Age]]

<br>The Present Age is sometimes known as the human age. It begun in the year 1 [[Records of Time|AH]], during what was known as the [[Advent of Humanity]] to the [[Elves]]. It was the arrival of the first [[Humans]] on the continent of [[Akora]]. It is considered one of the worst time periods in the history of Akora. 
It included many calamitous events such as the [[Destruction of Sylhserin]] in 294 [[Records of Time|AH]] and the [[Goblin Wars]] of 304 [[Records of Time|AH]].
