Thaumara is a magocratic society led by a council of the most powerful mages known as [[The Council of Seven]]. 

#### History
##### Formation
Thaumara was first formed in 4 AE by a small group of mages who did not wish to be under the rule of [[King Alvin Howsen]]. It is one of the original human settlements that was formed when they arrived on Akora. It initially began as a singular city state, now the capital, Nythalor, but quickly grew to a small kingdom. Over the past 200 years it has grown exponentially after a surge of magical discoveries exponentially grew the magocratic republic. 
Thaumara was formed in the south-east of Akora due to it's high concentration of [[Flowing Mana]]. 

##### Historic Events
- 4 AE - [[Nythalor]] is formed as an independent city state by a small group of mages. 
- 6 AE - The council of seven is formed. 
- 26 AE - Zarthuun, a small city state on the northern coast agrees to fall under the jurisdiction of the council of seven after a lengthy negotiation. The council of seven decides on a new name for their newly formed Magocratic republic, calling it Thaumara. 
- 48 AE - Both Selvaris and Orrisfall join the republic further growing Thaumara.  
- 94 AE - [[Cadea Mageson]] forms [[Cadea, Academy of Magics]]. It will eventually become the most prestigious school of magic on the continent of Akora. 
- 130-240 AE - Multiple settlements founded in the southern regions leading up to [[The Barren Lands]].

#### Economy
##### Economic System
The currency used throughout Thaumara is called a tuor (‘Tuor’ comes from the Latin word quatuor, meaning four). A tuor is a small coin, about 26mm in diameter (1 inch), made of a highly mana conductive metal called [[Glistening Silver]]. A tuor is unique in the fact that it has the ability to be split into smaller pieces and then re-combined later. These smaller pieces are known has a ‘half tuor’ and a ‘minor tuor’, with each being a tuor split in half and split into fourths respectively. 
These smaller pieces can be merged with any other coin’s pieces of the same size, meaning you can take two different half tuors from two different tuors and merge them together, but you cannot merge a half tuor and a minor tuor. 
When held together the pieces of a tuor will automatically snap together due to the dense amount of mana stored in [[Glistening Silver]]. It is also worth noting that the coins will always snap into perfect halves and fourths due to a technique in the creation if the coins called [[mana creasing]]. 
Tuors are created by a branch of the [[Great Tower]] called the treasury department. They create the coins as well as distribute them to the people of Thaumara.

#### Religion

The vast majority of Thaumarites follow [[Nehelia]], the goddess of magic. The second most popular belief is known commonly as [[The Fallen Ones]]. [[The Fallen Ones]] are people who believe the gods are no longer alive or in power. This is most commonly a human belief leftover from [[The Death of the Gods]], a precursor event to the [[Advent of Humanity]], although it has been growing more popular as of late. 

##### Primary Religions
- [[Followers of Nehelia]]
	- Nehelia is the goddess of magic. 
- [[The Fallen Ones]]
	- Followers of this religion believe that the gods died in [[The Death of the Gods]]
- Beast-kin religion
	- An ancient beast-kin religion that is still followed, albeit rarely.

##### Religious Issues
Tensions are frequently high between the Followers of [[Nehelia]] and [[The Fallen Ones]]. 
Although these religions are frequently at each other's throats, no major clashes have happened yet.

#### Magic
Thaumara is a republic who's foundation is built upon magic. It was formed by a group of mages and still is ruled under a council of mages decided on by the strongest mages of the era. The majority of their exports as a country revolve around magic making it a key factor in their society as a whole. 

##### Teachings of Magic
Thaumara is known as the most prestigious magic teaching location on the continent of Akora, with several magic schools around the republic as well as the most famous, [[Cadea, Academy of Magics]]. Young mages come from all across the continent to study in one of the many schools of Thaumara.

#### Executive
##### System of Government
Thaumara is ruled by the [[The Council of Seven|Council of Seven]]. The Council of Seven was established in 6 AE by the seven most powerful mages of the time. [[The Council of Seven]] is located in the [[Great Tower]] which houses other smaller governmental subsidiaries.

##### Unique Laws
Every person of the age of 9 will undertake a mandatory mana examination to test their mana capability. 

##### Law Enforcement
Guards are located in each city. While some are mages it is not a requirement to join the guard, however to become a captain or higher, being a mage is required. 
These guards uphold the laws of Thaumara and are under the jurisdiction of the [[Great Tower]]

#### Topography

##### Landscape
Thaumara can be split into two different climates. The north-east half of Thaumara is a tropical region, with warm temperatures and high rainfall. The south-east of Thaumara is a more arid and dry grasslands.

##### Natural Resources
Thaumara is most famous for it's high levels of flowing mana which pours from the mana waterfalls of the north-eastern regions. This in turn causes the abundance of [[Glistening Silver]], another major resource of Thaumara.
Thaumara is also full of lush tropical forests and many different fruits in the north-east.

##### Location Affecting Culture
Thaumarian cities are typically built around areas of abundant mana, with flowing mana springs or flows being prevalent focuses in the layout of towns and cities.
Along with this many festivals are hosted around the effects of flowing mana (More about this further below).

#### Inhabitants
##### Races
Thaumara is a region that is open to all races. Race does not give any advantage in any facet as what's more important is magical capability in Thaumara.
Many important officials of Thaumara vary in races from Humans, to Elves, to Beast Races. No job is gated by race. 

##### Traits
Thaumarites commonly have darker skin. In the south-west Beast-race Thaumarites commonly have lighter tan colored fur (though this is not a rule by any means).

##### Fauna and Flora
The north-east of Thaumara is home to many tropical species of plants and trees. The south-west is a more savannah like region, with arid grasslands and plains.

#### Culture

##### Features / Differences
Thaumara has been ruled by the Council or Seven since it’s inception. Thaumara is a heavily magic focused nation, known for its prominent teaching of magic and prevalence in the advancement of magical techniques and practices. It has birthed some of the greatest mages of not only the current era, but of all eras of history. 
Thaumara has a very "free flowing" culture, so to speak. It's open to anything, very few things are restricted. Magic is able to be freely practiced in the streets, relations are not limited by gender or race. (Within reason, laws still exist :D)

##### Main Art / Literature / Construction / Holidays
###### The Festival of Light
The night where the flowing mana glows the brightest called the Festival of Light is celebrated with a festival every year. Flowing mana reflects the light of the stars the strongest on the night of the summer solstice. 
The Festival of Light is celebrated with lots of alcohol and a magical fireworks show. 

##### Conflicts
The south west of Thaumara has been plagued with raids by clans of goblins and trolls. Often times these raids don’t amount to much but caught off guard the smaller villages in the south have been severely affected. In recent times mages have been dispatched to protect these towns.
