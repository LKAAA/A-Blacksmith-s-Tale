Yenthas, the hidden kingdom of the elves.

Its basically a big expansive city hidden in a giant forest.

Massive tree in the center of the city that is hidden from the outside world with magic. It overflows with [[Flowing Mana]] in waterfalls that come off of it's branches.

Ruled by a elder council
high focus on magic
They welcome outsiders who come upon them but do not seek interaction. The elders do a kind of background check your soul to decide if you can stay if you want to stay after stumbling upon them. Require special permission.
