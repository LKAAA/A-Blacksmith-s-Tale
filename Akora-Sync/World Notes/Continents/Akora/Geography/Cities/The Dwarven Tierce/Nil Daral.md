Nil Daral
Alternate Names:
The Great City of the Dwarves
The Great Kingdom of the Dwarves


The kings come from a long line named Lightbreaker. Long because its been like 2000 years not because there was a lot of them.
The king is currently [[Othir Lightbreaker the 3rd]]. Quite the name for someone so constantly underground. 
Massive underground city. There is only one main pathway to the surface (many hidden passages). They are open to visitors unlike the elven kingdom. Non-dwarves can live there but it is still over 90% dwarven population from the olden days (non-dwarves is a more recent thing).
They are at relative peace with the humanoid kingdoms.
