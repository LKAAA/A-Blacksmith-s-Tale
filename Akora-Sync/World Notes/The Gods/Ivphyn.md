Ivphyn is the Goddess of law, order, sky, light. Queen of the Heavens.
She is married to Ydis, God of peace, art, music, theater, poetry, writing.
They have three children: Lunis, Puella, and Pelus. 

The [[God Empress of the Setting Sun]] pretends to be Ivphyn who descended down to [[Akora]] from the heavens to lead the people of [[Ivpheria|Ivpheria]], but this is a false god.
