Nekojin are agile and graceful beings, blending humanoid forms with feline features. Nekojin have pointed ears, long tails, and eyes that gleam with mischief and wit. They are creatures of instinct and intuition, often relying on their keen senses to navigate the world around them. Nekojin are known for their lithe and slim bodies that allow for greater agility than normal, and a keen intelligence. Nekojin possess a fierce loyalty to their kin and are known for their strong sense of community, often banding together to face challenges and protect their territories with unwavering determination.
