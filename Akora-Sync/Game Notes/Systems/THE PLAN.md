#### Mechanics
- Minigames are fun for a base gameloop
	- Give ways to automate them easily
	- The player WILL get bored
	- the fun should not be IN THE MINIGAMeS ALWAYS
		- The minigames should be fun ofc, but they will ALWAYS get boring after doing them for 100s of times
- It is really fun to walk around and gather materials
	- Literally just picking up shit is fun
- It is really cool to see towns super alive
	- System of Important NPCS and Regular NPCS
		- Regular NPCs are randomly generated and just wander around. You can talk to them but they dont do much other than make the world feel alive (important with a bigger town than say Stardew Valley which is small enough that all the NPCs can be interactable)
		- Important NPCs, are the stardew valley npcs of the game. They also wander around on their schedules, but they are custom made have unique dialogue and interactions, can be relationshiped, have quests events, etc
- Running a shop is cool
	- 2 parts to running the main blacksmith shop
		- Custom Orders
			- Npcs will come by and place an order for specific weapons to craft.
			- You can choose to accept these if you think you can make them in the specified amount of time
			- might lose reputation if declined (although this sounds like it might be unfun)
			- Orders are for weapon/tool types you already know how to make (recipe known)
			- But you don't have to have ingredients on hand (why they give you a time / you give them a time)
		- Walk ins
			- This is the shop front. You can choose what tools/weapon/items you want to display and sell.
			- Customers will walk in and pick something up to purchase it.
			- These items for sale are items you make and put up, put the price, etc
				- Maybe you intentionally make them to have a bunch up, or you were practicing skills and made a bunch and put them up for sell
				- Craft same weapon multiple times to improve that recipe?
