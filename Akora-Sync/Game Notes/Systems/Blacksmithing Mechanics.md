## **Overview**
This is the basis and central set of mechanics of the game. Gather resources to craft weapons and tools. Sell those weapons and tools, or simply use them.
### **Core Blacksmithing Loop**

- To work with each metal, your forge must reach the required heat, and you need the appropriate tools or efficiency level. (Upgrade them!)
- Every metal has a different melting point.
- Keep the heat at the perfect temperature (Similar to _Stardew Valley_ fishing).
- Point scale system: Each forged weapon part adds different points to different stats (Durability, Strength, Sharpness, Beauty, etc.).
- Players can mix and match different weapon parts to create custom weapons.
    - Example: Combine a Mithril Axe Head (80 Durability, 70 Strength, 90 Sharpness, 40 Beauty) with a Mahogany Hilt and Dragon Scale Handle, etched with a Rune of Strength and the Forest.

## **1. Pre-Blacksmithing: Material Gathering**

### **Ore Mining**

- **Mechanic Type**: Resource Harvesting
- **Gameplay**:
    - Players use a pickaxe to mine ore.
    - Ore deposits are found in caves, and in some other locations, breaking them yields resources.

### **Wood Cutting**

- **Mechanic Type**: Resource Harvesting
- **Gameplay**:
    - Players chop trees with an axe.

### **Leather Gathering**

- **Mechanic Type**: Resource Harvesting
- **Gameplay**:
    - Players skin animals and creatures to obtain raw hides.
    - A knife is required

### **Merchant System**

- **Mechanic Type**: Economy & Trade
- **Gameplay**:
    - Buy materials from vendors instead of gathering them.

## **2. Tanning (Leather Processing)**

- **Mechanic Type**: Timed Processing
- **Gameplay**:
    - Players place raw hides on a curing rack.
    - Must wait a long duration 
    - Leather becomes cured after time has passed.

## **3. Handle Making (Wood Crafting)**

- **Mechanic Type**: UI Based Crafting System
- **Gameplay**:
    - Players take logs to a woodworking bench.
    - Choose from a list of handles (some require leather/other materials).

## **4. Blacksmithing (Core Gameplay Loop)**

### **Forge Management**

- **Mechanic Type**: Resource & Heat Management
- **Gameplay**:
    - Add fuel to the forge (coal by default, other fuels for special materials).
    - Pump bellows to maintain heat in the correct range.
    - If heat drops too low, metal will stop heating correctly. (No adverse effects besides a waste of time)

### **Metal Heating**

- **Mechanic Type**: Timed Processing
- **Gameplay**:
    - Place ore/metal in the forge and wait for it to heat.
    - Different metals have different heating times.

### **Forging (Anvil & Hammering)**

- **Mechanic Type**: Precision Mini-Game
- **Gameplay**:
    - Use tongs to move metal from forge to anvil.
    - Choose a weapon/tool part (e.g., sword blade, axe head).
    - **Mini-Game**: **Rhythm Hammering**
        - Hit metal to the beat to shape it properly.
        - Better timing = higher quality.
        - no song needed just click when it reaches the right place

### **Cooling (Quenching)**

- **Mechanic Type**: Resource & Timing-Based
- **Gameplay**:
    - Move forged part to a cooling barrel.
    - Different liquids (water, oil, or magical fluids) affect durability.
    - Certain materials require magical fluids

## **5. Assembling**

- **Mechanic Type**: Simple UI-Based Crafting
- **Gameplay**:
    - Interact with bench, select recipe
	- Take all forged pieces and other materials to the assembly table.
    - opens up a window with a center work area and all of the parts on the sides
    - Can drag each item around 
    - Have to drag item into the correct spot
    - Higher score based on how well you do

## **6. Optional: Magic Enhancement**

### **Mana Imbuing**

- **Mechanic Type**: Timed Enchantment
- **Gameplay**:
    - Place completed weapon/tool in a **Flowing Mana** pool.
    - Leave it in for a set duration to absorb mana.

### **Etching (Runes)**

- **Mechanic Type**: Freehand Drawing Mini-Game
- **Gameplay**:
    - Take imbued weapon/tool to an etching table.
    - **Mini-Game**: **Freehand Etching**
        - Draw a rune by tracing the pattern.
        - The closer the match, the stronger the enchantment.
    - Players unlock new runes through books, quests, or experimentation.

## **Supporting Mechanics**

- **Inventory System**: Tracks raw materials, tool parts, and completed weapons.
- **Progression System**: Unlocks better crafting techniques, fuels, and enchantments.
- **Durability System**: Poorly crafted weapons break faster, encouraging mastery.
- **Interactable Objects**

### **Blacksmithing Systems**

Blacksmithing is divided into multiple facets, each contributing to different aspects of crafting equipment:

1. **Metalwork** – Smelting ore into bars, forging metal into weapon parts, heating forge.
2. **Leatherwork** – Curing leather and turning it into grips/handles.
3. **Woodwork** – Crafting wooden handles and other parts.
4. **Rune Etching** – Engraving magical runes into weapons, requiring liquid mana.
5. **Assembling** – Combining all parts into a finished weapon.

---

### **Magi-tech & Automation**

- **Magi-tech: Magic-imbued mechanical objects that automate or enhance blacksmithing. Expensive due to the need for rare magical crystals.
- Used for:
    - **Automated Bellows** (Maintains forge temperature).
    - **Automated Etcher** (Etches runes automatically).
- NPC can upgrade blacksmithing tools (bellows, forge, anvil, etc.) using “Magic Imbued Crystals” and money.

### **Minigames & Mechanics to Prototype**

- **Furnace Heating System:**
    - Ore goes into the furnace, takes time to heat.
    - Heated bar can cool passively or be dunked in water for instant cooling.
    - Forging can only be done with a heated bar. (Cooled bars can be reheated).
    - Multiple furnaces provide efficiency.

- **Hammering Minigame:**
    - Rhythm-based (hit hammer to the beat).
    - Higher forging level = bigger hit window, better quality.

- **Assembling Weapons:**
    - Dragging pieces together like a puzzle.
    - Potential assistant/automation upgrade.

- **Rune Etching:**
    - Physically draw rune patterns.
    - Closer to the design = better quality etching.

- **Forge Upkeep:**
    - Bellows attached to the furnace, must be used to maintain heat.
    - Fuel (coal) required.
    - Upgrade options: **Auto bellows, longer-lasting fuel types**.

- **Sound Effects & Satisfaction:**
    - Dunking finished blades in water should make a satisfying _shhhhssss_ sound.
