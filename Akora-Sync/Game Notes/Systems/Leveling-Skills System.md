## **Overview**
This is the second core of the game. You can level basically anything that you do, to improve the speed of it, and unlock special perks for it. 

## **Similarities**
This leveling system is most similar to WoW's leveling system, combined with Stardew Valley.

## **Available Skills**
- Mining
	- Ways to gain XP: 
		- Mining ore
		- Mining Other things
- Foraging
	- Ways to gain XP:
		- Chopping wood
		- Gathering plants
- Skinning
	- Ways to gain XP:
		- Skinning animals/Creatures
- Leatherworking
	- Ways to gain XP:
		- Tanning leather (When you pick it up off the rack)
		- Crafting with leather
- Crafting
	- Ways to gain XP:
		- Crafting handles
		- Crafting other things
- Metalworking
	- Ways to gain XP:
		- Smelting ore
		- Crafting metal tool/weapon parts
- Assembling
	- Ways to gain XP:
		- Assembling tools/weapons
- Etching
	- Ways to gain XP:
		- Etching runes into weapons
- Cooking
	- Ways to gain XP:
		- Cooking
