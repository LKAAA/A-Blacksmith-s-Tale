“Duchess!”

I turned towards the call, my long dress swishing across the polished stone floors. 

“We have just received a report from Frostspire,” he said, stopping abruptly in front of me. He held up a loose stack of papers. 

“Thank you,” I grabbed the stack and started sorting through them while turning to walk towards my office. Business as usual it seems. They had a slight population increase this month, we might need to send more resources. I need to get Delric to look into it. 

“Duchess, the messenger had one more message from Count Selvan, but I was instructed to tell you that it was for your eyes only.” 

I turned, my eyebrows raising slightly. He pulled from his back pocket a small envelope with house Selvan’s Seal. I grabbed the letter from him. It's heavier than I thought it would be.

“Thank you.” 

He bowed low and began walking away. I made my way to my office still sorting through the first report. Nothing unusual throughout. What could the letter be about then? Count Selvan doesn’t often correspond in secret like this. 

Reaching my office I entered and sat down at my desk. I carefully broke the seal on the letter and gently pulled it out of the envelope.
