
“Witch.”

“No mother, please.”

“You foul witch! You brought this curse upon us!”

“No I didn’t mother, it wasn’t me—”

The harsh slap cut Reina’s words off short. Her eyes began to water as she brought her hand up to her stinging cheek. 

“M-mother,” she whimpered. 

Her mother raises her hand again rearing back for another slap, her eyes wide and crazed. She let out a horrid screech that reverberated in Reina’s ears. Reina stumbled backwards avoiding the blow. Off balance for the missed slap her mother fumbled forwards, her foot catching on a chair as she fell on the floor with a thud. 

Reina stared down at her, hand still clutching her cheek. She took a step back. Her mother struggled to rise to her feet again. Reina took another step back, her left hand brushing the door behind her. Her mother, regaining her feet, staggered forward again. Reina fumbled with the door behind her struggling to find the handle. 

“You will pay you… vile… sinful WITCH!”

The door fell open and Reina with it. She stumbled to her feet as her mother emerged in the doorway, hands still raised, raring to strike. Tears streaming down her face she ran, not even daring a glance back. 

-----

Reina struggled to catch her breath. Her deep breaths were intertwined with tearful hiccups. She had stopped at a cross in the road. The forest surrounded her, each path seemingly only leading to darkness. 

She touched her cheek. It still stung. Her eyes started welling up with tears again. She closed her eyes in the hope that it would stop. Deep breaths she told herself, but it was too late, the tears were already starting to fall again. She fell to her knees unable to stay standing for any longer. 

“Why mother? I didn’t do anything to father,” she whimpered to herself. “I-I didn’t mean for this to happen.”
