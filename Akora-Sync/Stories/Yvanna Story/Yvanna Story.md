### Chapter 1

 For as long as Yvanna could remember she had been working in the kitchen. It was hard work, but rewarding for her. She quite enjoyed her time there, and the head cook was very kind to her as well. Her early years were spent washing dishes and thoroughly cleaning the kitchen, but what she enjoyed the most was helping her mother cook meals for the maids and staff of the house. She would take her hand and guide her as Yvanna learned to peel vegetables, to slice bread, or to cut the meats. 

Yvanna had just recently turned 16. This is an important age as it marks a young lady's coming of age. But this is really only important for a noble’s daughter, one of pure blood that is. For Yvanna, though born of the Duke, and hence noble blood, her mother was that of a common born maid. Well in a way turning 16 was also important for her as this was when she learned of her birth, but learning that didn’t change much at all about her life. 

But she didn’t really mind that much. Working in the kitchen was fulfilling, especially since she was allowed to help in the actual cooking now, but maybe there was a bit of hope deep in her heart that maybe her father would send for her. But then again, she wasn’t daft, she knew it wasn’t actually going to happen. I mean he had mother and her working in his kitchen for the past 16 years and during that time she had barely even seen him. He might not even know she exists for all she knew. But Yvanna didn’t let any of this discourage her. She kept working hard, helping the kitchen in any way she could and slowly learning the different intricacies of cooking. 

“Yvanna can you grab the vegetables for today?” 

“On it!” Yvanna cheerfully responded. She whistled a little tune as she walked over to the storage room sorting through a dozen or so crates of different ingredients until she found the carrots, cabbage, and onions. 

“Oh and grab a few cloves of garlic too.” 

“K’!” With a grunt of effort she lifted the crate of vegetables, it was heavy but not so much that she couldn’t manage. Working in the kitchen had its bonuses after all, she was a fair bit stronger than the average noblewoman. She grabbed a few cloves of garlic from the shelf before she made her way out of the storage room. 

“Here you go miss’.” She dropped off the cloves of garlic before continuing on.

“Thanks Yvanna.” 

The head cook was a cheerful woman who Yvanna quite liked. She didn’t like to mention this to anyone but Yvanna couldn’t remember what her name actually was, everyone had been calling her ‘miss’ and ‘head cook’ for too long. Still she was a kind woman who, while strict, was always helping Yvanna and teaching her whatever she needed.  
She reached the vegetable counter and pushed the crate up on top. Rolling up her sleeves she grabbed a few carrots and cabbages out and started cutting. The rhythmic technique was quite soothing and enjoyable to her, and she’d gotten quite good at it, a far cry from how she used to be. Her fingers tinged a little remembering all the cuts she had given herself from when she first learned. 

Chop chop chop chop.

“S’cuse me, careful’ behind you. Hot pan here!” Yvanna quickly pressed herself up against the counter making herself small. That was Lissandra, one of the other maids that worked in the kitchen. She had dirty blonde hair that was always tied up in a loose bun on the top of her head. She was older than Yvanna and they had developed somewhat of a sisterly relationship over the years. Yvanna had practically grown up with her after all. Lissandra was in charge of baking the bread, and for good reason. Her bread was the most delicious Yvanna had ever tasted. 

“Oh wait, have you seen my mother around? I haven’t seen her since this morning,” said Yvanna.

“Did she not tell you?” said Lissandra spinning back around to face her pan held high over her head. “Something about a missing shipment of eggplants, the head maid sent her out to deal with it.” 

“Alright then thank ya.” Yvanna shrugged and went back to chopping cabbages. It wasn’t often mother was sent out of the kitchen but it was even less often that a shipment of eggplants gets lost. 

Chop chop chop chop.

“Lunch time!” Yvanna exclaimed. 

“You always look so excited for lunch,” chuckled Katie. 

“Well yeah who doesn’t love food,” replied Yvanna as she stretched out her back. She let out a multitude of sighs as she bent back, her joints cracking audibly.

“You’re too young to be making that much noise stretching Yvanna,” said Katie. “You’re making me feel old.”

Katie was another maid that worked in the kitchen. She was about a head shorter than Yvanna but a few years older. Katie had begun working in the kitchen only a few months prior but they had quickly become friends spending most of their free time together.

“Yeah yeah, complain about your old bones over lunch.”

They started heading to the staff room to eat their lunch but a unique sight caught Yvanna’s eye. At the doorway to the kitchen on the far side of the room stood the head chef talking to a thinly built man. He was nearly two heads taller than the head chef, not that that was saying much as she barely came up to Yvanna’s shoulders, and Yvanna was of average height. The man had short cut black hair and a long nose with a pair of thin wired glasses perched atop them. 

Yvanna had only seen this man once before but she recognized him instantly. It was Claude, the head attendant of the entire palace. As well as being head of the palace he also served as butler of the duke.

She had only ever seen him once in the kitchen a few years ago. He didn’t frequently attend to matters in this area of the house himself. Any messages he would have for the kitchen staff was generally passed through the head maid. 

“You comin’ Yvanna?” 

She didn’t even realize she had stopped.  
“Yeah, coming.” She pulled her gaze from them and continued walking to the staff room. 
  

------

The staff room was a relatively big room. It had a serving table on one end next to the door leading to the kitchen. Numerous wooden tables stood in the middle with many maids and servants chatting and gossiping away. Two large windows adorned the left wall letting in natural light from the outside. It was functional but definitely not comfortable, which Yvanna assumed was to entice people to go back to work as opposed to hanging around in the staff room. 

Together Yvanna and Katie both served themselves and sat at the close table. Today was one of Yvanna’s favorites. A nice bowl of beef stew and some delicious bread baked by Lissandra herself. 

“Delicious!” exclaimed Yvanna. 

“You really love beef stew don’t ya.” 

“Come on, you know it’s delicious too.”

Katie laughed. “Yeah it is, you’re right.”

The door behind Yvanna opened and curiosity and surprise lit up Katie’s face. Yvanna turned. Behind her in the door frame stood Claude. He took a step forward into the room. His dark eyes gazed around the room until finally falling onto Yvanna.

“You. Come with me. The Duke has summoned you.”

“M-Me?” The shock was palpable in her voice. 

“You are Yvanna, yes?”

“Yes but-“

“Then come with me.” Without even waiting for her to answer he turned and left. 

The duke? Summoned me? What could he possibly need me for? 

“Yvanna? You need to go. He’s not gonna wait for you.” Katie’s voice snapped her out of her thoughts. 

“R-right.” Yvanna quickly got up from her seat and ran out of the room after Claude. He was already at the other end of the kitchen and she quickly hurried after him. 

They walked down hallways and up stairs Yvanna had never been on before. They entered the upper halls of the palace, a far cry from the servants floor that she had lived in her entire life. Though it was the same palace it was like she had entered a whole new world. The walls were brighter, the adornment practically glittering with jewels on every shelf and table. You could tell someone important lived in this area. The decorations in the halls alone were worth more than she and her mom combined had made in their lifetimes. 

They reached a large ornate wooden door. Claude knocked twice. 

“I have arrived with her, my lord.” 

A moment passed. 

“You may enter.” 

Claude opened the door and beckoned Yvanna in. Swallowing hard, she nervously took a step forward. The room she entered was a large room, its walls were decorated with paintings and statues. On the right were two small couches facing each other with a table in between. To the left of Yvanna was a small desk with papers and books stacked high on it. 

In the center was a second desk, but this one much larger than the other. Behind the desk sat her father, Duke Mercili. He was definitely a beautiful man. He had rich blond hair, bright blue, aqua colored eyes, and a perfect jawline. His broad shoulders were visible under the perfectly tailored suit that he wore. 

As Yvanna stood before him she tried to steady her breathing. She had seen him in passing a few times before but this was the first time she had ever been face to face with him. His eyes looked her up and down, studying her. They had this strange softness to them, while at the same time feeling a little cold and unapproachable. 

“You look like your mother.” His voice was cold and sharp, but still held a few gentle tones. 

Yvanna didn’t know how to respond. Until a few minutes ago she thought her father didn’t even know they existed, and now he was telling her she looked like her mother? She opened her mouth to speak but no sound came out. She closed it just as quickly. 

“I have decided that it is time for you to finally become my daughter,” he said. 

Yvanna’s mouth dropped open once again, shock evident on her face like a gal who is shocked. Her father cut off her incoherent stuttering. 

“That includes studying the etiquette of noble women, the history of our family, and finally marriage,” he continued, his eyes never leaving Yvanna’s face. A small smile entered his face. Well I guess you could call it a smile. It was more like the corners of his lips upturned a little. “In fact I have already chosen who you will marry. You will marry Count Vane of Frostguard.”

“Ye-yes sir,” Yvanna managed to say. It was hard to control her breathing. Her father was finally accepting her. The moment that she hadn't even dared to think about was happening so suddenly.

“From now on you will refer to me as father. Tomorrow, you will begin your studies early. Claude will guide you to your new room to rest in until then.”

“Thank you father,” squeaked Yvanna. She gave a quick curtsy as her mother had taught her before turning toward the door. As her hand reached the handle her father spoke again. 

“I have… high hopes for you.”

Yvanna’s cheeks grew hot as a smile emerged on her face. 

“Thank you father!” She slipped through the door and emerged back in the hall she had entered from, lightly closing the door behind her. 

Claude stood beside the door as she walked out. He quickly turned and started off down the hallway without so much as a glance to her. They arrived at a large door after a few minutes of walking. Claude opened it and beckoned her inside. The room was far bigger than any room she had ever stayed in. Two large windows adorned the back wall and in-between them stood a large bed. To the right when entering the room was a large dresser and a full length mirror, while on the left wall was a small couch and a desk with a matching chair. 

“You will be informed of where to go for your studies tomorrow. Until then you may rest.” 

“Okay, thank-,” started Yvanna, but Claude swiftly turned and stepped out, closing the door behind him. She heard his footsteps fading from outside as she stood there. 

She glanced around the room again. It was far fancier than any room she had ever been in. It looked to her like something only a princess would stay in. She walked over to the desk and ran her hands over it. Was this all truly real? Her father was finally accepting her as his daughter? Yvanna had deep down always wished this would happen but she never thought it would be possible. But here she was. A small smile began to flirt with her lips. 

She turned toward the excessively large bed behind her and stared at it for a moment. Then with a run she leapt onto it. She let out a laugh as she stared up at the ceiling above her. It was the most comfortable bed she had ever even touched, and she could not remember ever feeling happier.
